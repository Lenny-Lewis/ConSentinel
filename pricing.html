Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Does Sentinel introduce compute overhead?

No. Sentinel executes via kernel eBPF bytecode hooks, which typically consume less than 0.4% CPU and under 40MB of resident RAM per bare-metal node.

Can we run Sentinel in an air-gapped data center?

Yes. Our Enterprise tier ships as a self-contained container bundle with local cryptographic verification and offline threat database updates.

How does the 60-second key rotation operate?

Workloads exchange ephemeral Diffie-Hellman or post-quantum Kyber session tokens in-memory. In the event of a compromised secret, validity expires in under a minute.

How does node billing calculate for auto-scaled clusters?

We bill based on 95th-percentile active node hours across the billing cycle, preventing billing spikes during temporary traffic surges or batch workloads.
